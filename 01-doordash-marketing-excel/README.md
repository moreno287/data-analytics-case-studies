# DoorDash Marketing Campaign Analysis (Excel)

**Tools:** Excel · VLOOKUP · Pivot tables · Scatter plots · Bar charts · Exploratory data analysis

## Goal
Help the marketing team plan its next campaign by understanding who responded to past campaigns.

## What I did
- Analyzed real-world marketing campaign data **using only Excel**.
- Joined and enriched the data with VLOOKUPs, then summarized it with pivot tables.
- Explored customer demographics and campaign response with scatter plots and bar charts.
- Wrote a summary of the findings and recommendations for the marketing team.
