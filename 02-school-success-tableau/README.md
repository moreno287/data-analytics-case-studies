# Evaluating School Success in Massachusetts (Tableau)

**Tools:** Tableau · Dashboard design · KPIs · Data visualization · Stakeholder presentation

## Goal
Compare schools to understand what separates high-performing schools from the rest.

## What I did
- Built a Tableau dashboard comparing **~1,800 schools** across hundreds of features.
- Used KPIs, scatter plots, bar charts and area charts to show performance and its drivers.
- Presented the dashboard to stakeholders in a recorded video walkthrough.
