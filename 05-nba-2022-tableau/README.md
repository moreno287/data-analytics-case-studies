# NBA 2022 Season Analysis (Tableau)

**Tools:** Tableau · Tableau Story · Dashboarding · Written report

## Goal
Find patterns in player and team performance in the 2022 NBA season.

## What I did
- Analyzed 2022 NBA season statistics.
- Built stacked bar charts, heatmaps, treemaps and bubble charts.
- Combined them into a Tableau story, with a written report.
